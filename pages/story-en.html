Entrepreneurial Vision · From Soil to Algorithm

As a university student, I've traveled between the laboratory and the fields, witnessing firsthand the significant need for precise decision-making in traditional agriculture. In 2024, we began introducing the latest AI technologies into pest monitoring and yield prediction, aiming to provide farmers and agricultural enterprises with real-time decision support through low-cost, highly reliable intelligent tools. Our team has formed a compact structure combining algorithms, embedded systems, and agricultural science.

Perspective on the AI Industry

The wave of large language models should not remain confined to conversations and generation; it should penetrate the "last mile" of industry. Agricultural scenarios involve complex data and numerous variables, requiring lightweight, robust AI solutions. We adhere to the principle of "vertical penetration first, horizontal expansion later," focusing on solving real pain points rather than chasing concepts.

Pain Points We Address

· Delayed pest identification & misjudgment leading to yield loss → Our edge AI box can identify issues in real-time and provide treatment recommendations.
· Yield estimation relying on experience → Based on satellite + drone multimodal data, we build time-series prediction models with over 20% accuracy improvement.
· High cost of digital farm record-keeping → Self-developed low-code forms + AI voice input, creating a data-driven decision loop.

Key Technical Progress

Accumulated agricultural visual dataset (excluding sensitive geographic information) exceeding 50,000 images, model mAP reached 86.7%.
Edge inference framework adapted for RK3588 and Jetson series, end-to-end latency < 200ms.
Reached pilot cooperation intentions with two provincial agricultural cooperatives, field deployment feedback is positive.
Due to confidentiality agreements, specific algorithm architecture and key data are not publicly available; detailed discussion available after signing NDA offline.

Entrepreneurial Philosophy

Practical implementation, no concept chasing, focus on commercialization. Every algorithm improvement must translate into actual field benefits. We promise early investors will receive priority access and deep technical synchronization.
